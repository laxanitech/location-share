Location link

Get a share link

When someone opens your link and allows location access, you’ll get their coordinates and a Google Maps link.

  1. Create a private channel name below (keep it secret).
  2. Open your ntfy inbox on your phone to receive the ping.
  3. Send them the generated share link.

They will see a normal browser permission prompt. GPS cannot be read without their OK.
